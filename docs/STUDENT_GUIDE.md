# Student Guide - How to Use Your Profile & Take Exams

## Welcome!

This guide explains all the features available to you as a student in the exam system.

---

## Getting Started

### Logging In
1. Go to the login page
2. Enter your **username** (provided by your instructor)
3. Enter your **password** (provided by your instructor)
4. Click **Login**

💡 **Tip**: Bookmark the login page for quick access next time

---

## Student Dashboard

### What You See:
After logging in, you'll see your Student Dashboard with two main sections:

#### 1. Available Exams
A list of exams assigned to you by your instructor:
- Exam title
- Status (Active = you can take it now)
- "Start Exam" button to begin

#### 2. My Results
Your past exam scores:
- Exam name
- Date taken
- Score you received
- Duration

### Actions Available:

**View Exams**: Click "Start Exam" to take an exam

**View Results**: See all your past exam scores and dates

**Go to Profile**: Click **Profile** button (top right) to edit your information

**Logout**: Click **Logout** when done

---

## Your Profile Settings - NEW!

### Accessing Your Profile

1. From Student Dashboard, click **Profile** button (top right)
2. You'll see your profile settings page

### What You Can Edit:

✅ **First Name** - Your given name
✅ **Last Name** - Your family name  
✅ **Email** - Contact email address

### What You CANNOT Edit:

❌ **Username** - This is permanent and cannot be changed
❌ **Password** - Locked for security (contact your instructor if you need to change it)

### How to Update Your Profile:

1. Click the field you want to edit
2. Clear the old text and type new information
3. Multiple fields can be edited at once
4. Click **"Save Changes"** button (bottom right)
5. Wait for green success message
6. Changes are saved! ✅

### Example: Changing Your Name

**Before:**
```
First Name: John
Last Name: Doe
Email: john@example.com
```

**Steps:**
1. Click First Name field
2. Clear "John" and type "Juan"
3. Click Last Name field  
4. Clear "Doe" and type "Smith"
5. Click "Save Changes"
6. See "Profile updated successfully!" message

**After:**
```
First Name: Juan
Last Name: Smith
Email: john@example.com
```

## Taking Exams

### Starting an Exam

1. Go to Student Dashboard
2. In "Available Exams" section, find your exam
3. Click **"Start Exam"** button

### If Exam Has PIN Code - NEW!

Some exams require a PIN code for security:

**What You'll See:**
1. PIN verification modal appears
2. Prompt: "Enter exam PIN code"
3. PIN input field

**What to Do:**
1. Your instructor will give you the PIN code
2. Enter the PIN exactly as they say it
3. Click **"Submit"** or press Enter
4. If correct ✅ → Questions load
5. If wrong ❌ → Error message, try again

**PIN Example:**
```
Instructor says: "The PIN is 5748"
You type: 5748
You press: Enter or Submit
Result: Exam questions appear!
```

### Answering Questions

1. Read each question carefully
2. Select your answer (radio button or checkbox)
3. Move to next question
4. Answer all questions you can
5. Review your answers before submitting

**Tips for Success:**
- ⏰ Watch the timer (top right)
- 📝 Read all options before choosing
- ⚠️ Don't refresh - you'll lose progress
- 💾 Answers are saved as you go
- ✅ Double-check before submitting

### Submitting Your Exam

1. Answer all questions
2. Review your work (optional)
3. Click **"Submit Exam"** button
4. Confirm submission when asked
5. See your score immediately!

### After Submitting

✅ Exam is complete
✅ Score is recorded
✅ You're taken back to dashboard
✅ Result appears in "My Results" section

## My Results - Enhanced View

### Viewing Past Exams

1. On Student Dashboard, scroll to **"My Results"**
2. See table with all completed exams:
   - **Exam Name** - The title of exam (now shows correctly!)
   - **Score** - Your points earned
   - **Date** - When you took it

### Understanding Your Results

**Example:**
```
Exam Name: Final Math Exam
Score: 85/100
Date: 2024-01-15
Status: Submitted
```

This means:
- You took the "Final Math Exam"
- You scored 85 points out of 100 (85%)
- You completed it on January 15, 2024

### If You See "Unknown Exam"

This shouldn't happen anymore, but if you do:
1. Refresh the page (F5 key)
2. If still showing, contact your instructor
3. The name should appear after a few seconds

## Troubleshooting

### "I Can't Find My Exam"
**Check:**
- Are you logged in correctly?
- Has your instructor assigned this exam to you?
- Is the exam marked as "Active"?
- Try refreshing your page (F5)

### "PIN Modal Won't Close"
**Check:**
- Is PIN correct? (instructor should confirm)
- No spaces or extra characters?
- Try again - PIN is case-sensitive
- Contact instructor if you have wrong PIN

### "I Can't See Questions"
**Check:**
- Did you enter PIN correctly? (if required)
- Is the exam loading? (wait a few seconds)
- Try refreshing the page
- Check your internet connection
- Contact instructor if problem persists

### "My Score Isn't Showing"
**Check:**
- Did you click "Submit Exam"? (not just closing tab)
- Wait 5-10 seconds for score to process
- Refresh your page
- Check "My Results" section carefully
- Contact instructor if still missing

### "Profile Changes Won't Save"
**Check:**
- Are you connected to internet?
- Did you click "Save Changes" button?
- Are all required fields filled?
- Wait for green success message
- Try again or contact instructor
